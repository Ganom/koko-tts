Koko TTS Voices

How to Use TTS During Streams

There are two ways to make the bot read your messages with these voices:

Cheer: Type "Cheer200 [voicename] your message"
Channel Points: Redeem a highlighted message and start with "[voicename] your message"

Voice Commands

You can also set a default voice for your messages:

!setvoice: Type "!setvoice voicename" to set your default voice
!clearvoice: Type "!clearvoice" to remove your default voice
